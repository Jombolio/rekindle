**Pre-release.** The fourth beta of 1.6.4, polishing Android's read aloud. Includes everything from earlier betas.

## Changes (Android)

- **Cover and position on the lock screen:** while a book is read aloud, the lock screen and the notification show its cover and its title, with where you are underneath: "Chapter 6 of 62 · Page 3 of 14" in the page and two-page layouts, or how far through the chapter in the scroll layouts.
- **"Read from here":** the selection menu's item for reading aloud from selected text is now called **Read from here**. Phones with Google's speech services add their own **Read aloud** item to every app, which reads just the selected text, so the two had the same name.

## Upgrading

- **Android:** install the APK below.
- **Desktop and server:** no changes since beta.1 beyond the version number.

Every download except the Android APK has a `.sha256` next to it.
