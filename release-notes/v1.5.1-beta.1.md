**Pre-release.** The first beta of 1.5.1, with a big rework of text books. It hasn't had much real-world use yet, so please report anything odd, especially with your own EPUB and Kindle files.

## New

- **Text books open reliably, and read properly** (desktop and Android). Images and the book's own styles now show, links between chapters work, and your place is kept within a chapter, including through font and theme changes. A book only counts as finished near the end of its last chapter.
- **MOBI, AZW and AZW3 books can be read.** The server converts them to EPUB the first time they're opened, including KF8 files. DRM-protected books can't be opened, and the reader says why.
- **Books stream a chapter at a time**, like comic pages, so you can read without downloading first. Read-only accounts can now read text books; saving a whole book still needs the download permission.
- **Import Kindle books and EPUBs** (desktop, for accounts that can manage media). In the upload panel, pick a folder or a Kindle connected over USB to preview each book, upload the ready ones and see what was imported, skipped or failed. Rekindle doesn't remove DRM: protected and KFX books are reported and never uploaded.
- **A built-in English dictionary** that works offline. On desktop, select a word; on Android, long-press a word and tap **Define**. The meaning floats over the page, so your place doesn't move.
- **Choose the default font and text size for books**, in Settings or from the new font button in the reader.
- **Android can check for updates.** Settings → Updates has **Check for updates** and **Include pre-releases**. Copies from F-Droid are still updated by F-Droid.

## Fixes

- Desktop: closing the reader could save page 0 over your real progress, comics included.
- Desktop: the update zip stayed locked on Windows after it was extracted.

## Upgrading

Update the server first. Older servers can't stream books to these clients.

- **Server:** stop it, run `Rekindle.Server --dev-update`, start it. Your `appsettings.json`, `data/` folder and other files are left untouched. The database gains a column for your place within a chapter, so back up `data/` if you might go back to 1.5.0.
- **Desktop:** turn on **Receive pre-releases** in Settings → Updates, then **Check for updates**, or use the download below.
- **Android:** install the APK below; your app data is kept. From this version on, the app can check for updates itself. F-Droid builds are signed differently, so to try this beta you'd need to uninstall the F-Droid copy first, which removes your downloads and local progress on that device.

Every download has a `.sha256` next to it.
