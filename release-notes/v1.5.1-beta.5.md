**Pre-release.** The fifth beta of 1.5.1. It brings new ways to read text books, readability fonts and accessibility options, on desktop and Android. Everything from the earlier betas is here too: the text book rework, Kindle formats and import, the dictionary, Android's update check, in-app updates for Windows installs, the desktop book layout and reading progress fixes, and the default reading theme.

## New

- **Reading layouts for books.** Choose how a book is laid out:
  - **Scroll by chapter**, as before.
  - **Continuous scroll**, where the whole book is one scroll and each chapter follows the last.
  - **Single page**, a page at a time. On desktop, pages break between lines, never through one.
  - **Two pages**, a side-by-side spread for wide screens and landscape.

  In the page layouts, turn pages by tapping or clicking either side of the page, with the arrow keys, Page Up/Down or Space, the mouse wheel, a swipe on Android, or the arrows in the bottom bar. Turning past a chapter's last page opens the next. Your place carries across layouts and devices.
- **Readability fonts.** OpenDyslexic, Atkinson Hyperlegible and Lexend now come with the apps, under the SIL Open Font License. Their licences are under **Licences** in Settings.
- **Readability and accessibility options:**
  - line, letter, word and paragraph spacing
  - line width
  - left or justified alignment
  - two high-contrast themes
  - bold text
  - turning off page-turn animation
  - a reading ruler that keeps your place on the line you're reading
- **An Aa button in the reader** holds all of these. They're also under Settings → Books, and changes apply straight away.

## Upgrading

- **Desktop:** turn on **Receive pre-releases** in Settings → Updates, then **Check for updates**. On Windows, installs from the installer still on 1.5.0 or beta.1 need this version installed by hand once, from the download below.
- **Server:** no changes since beta.1. To move from 1.5.0, stop it, run `Rekindle.Server --dev-update`, start it, and back up `data/` first if you might go back to 1.5.0. Update the server before the clients: older servers can't stream books.
- **Android:** use Settings → Updates → **Check for updates** with **Include pre-releases** on, or install the APK below.

Every download except the Android APK has a `.sha256` next to it.
