**Pre-release.** The twelfth beta of 1.5.1, including everything from earlier betas.

## Fixes

- **No more colour flicker turning double pages:** in double-page reading, a page that stands alone — every landscape page, and the page just before one — made the turns next to it lift the whole spread from its outer edge on a grey sheet, which flashed against the black of every other turn. A spread now always turns about its centre spine, whatever follows it, with the next page arriving on the back of the turning half. Lone pages turn on black to match. On both desktop and Android.
- **Pages no longer shrink as a turn begins:** a scan smaller than the window was drawn one size in the reader and a smaller one as the page being turned. The turning page now matches the size it is shown at.
- **The margins hold still:** when the next page is bigger than the current one — a landscape page, or simply a scan of a different size — its edges no longer jump into the margins on the first frame of the turn. They fade in as the page turns.

## Upgrading

- **Desktop:** enable **Receive pre-releases**, then use Settings → Updates → **Check for updates**.
- **Android:** use Settings → Updates → **Check for updates** with pre-releases enabled, or install the APK.
- **Server:** no functional changes since beta.8. If upgrading from an earlier beta, update the server before the clients to use scheduled scans, read marks and tracking exclusions.

Every download except the Android APK has a `.sha256` next to it.
