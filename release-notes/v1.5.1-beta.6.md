**Pre-release.** The sixth beta of 1.5.1. It fixes the reading ruler and gives page turns a proper animation on desktop. Everything from the earlier betas is here too, including the reading layouts, readability fonts and accessibility options from [beta.5](https://git.jombo.uk/Jombo/rekindle/releases/tag/v1.5.1-beta.5).

## Fixes

- **Desktop: page turns slide.** In the Single page and Two pages layouts, the new page slides in as the old one slides out. Turning into the next or previous chapter slides too, and the page you're leaving stays up while the chapter loads instead of flashing to a spinner. Turn off **Page-turn animation** in the Aa panel for pages that change at once.
- **Desktop: the reading ruler follows the pointer everywhere.** It no longer stops over the margins beside the pages, over the top and bottom bars, or while a mouse button is held, such as when selecting text.

## Upgrading

- **Desktop:** turn on **Receive pre-releases** in Settings → Updates, then **Check for updates**. On Windows, installs from the installer still on 1.5.0 or beta.1 need this version installed by hand once, from the download below.
- **Server:** no changes since beta.1. To move from 1.5.0, stop it, run `Rekindle.Server --dev-update`, start it, and back up `data/` first if you might go back to 1.5.0. Update the server before the clients: older servers can't stream books.
- **Android:** no changes since beta.5. Use Settings → Updates → **Check for updates** with **Include pre-releases** on, or install the APK below.

Every download except the Android APK has a `.sha256` next to it.
