**Pre-release.** The fifth beta of 1.6.4. Includes everything from earlier betas.

## Fixes (Android)

- **The book reader's controls no longer sit under the phone's own bars:** the back button and chapter title were drawn under the clock and status icons, and the chapter arrows under the navigation buttons. The bars now sit clear of them, and the page stays inside them too, so scrolled text doesn't run under the clock. In landscape they also keep clear of side navigation buttons and camera cutouts.

## Upgrading

- **Android:** install the APK below.
- **Desktop and server:** no changes since beta.1 beyond the version number.

Every download except the Android APK has a `.sha256` next to it.
