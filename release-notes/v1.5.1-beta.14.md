**Pre-release.** The fourteenth beta of 1.5.1, including everything from earlier betas.

## New

- **Books read aloud (desktop):** text books can now be read to you with a natural voice, on your own computer. Download the voice once from Settings → Books (about 333 MB), then press the speech button in a book's top bar. The sentence being read is highlighted, pages turn with it, and reading carries on into the next chapter. Choose between three voices (Heart, Emma and George) in Settings or in the reader's Reading options, even mid-sentence. Works offline once downloaded. Not yet available in the continuous layout.
- **Type to search (desktop):** start typing while browsing a library or a series and the listing filters to match. Escape clears it. On by default; turn it off in Settings → Appearance.

## Upgrading

- **Desktop:** enable **Receive pre-releases**, then use Settings → Updates → **Check for updates**.
- **Android:** no changes since beta.12 beyond the version number.
- **Server:** no functional changes since beta.8. If upgrading from an earlier beta, update the server before the clients to use scheduled scans, read marks and tracking exclusions.

Every download except the Android APK has a `.sha256` next to it.
