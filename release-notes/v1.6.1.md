Rekindle 1.6.1 fixes a flicker when turning back a page on Android.

## Fixes

- **Turning back a page no longer flickers** (Android). With comic and manga page turns on, going back a page could flash the previous page across the whole screen, or a blank page, before it curled into place. The page you'd turn back to is now ready before the turn starts, as the page ahead always was. This applies to single-page, double-page and right-to-left reading.

## Upgrading

- **Android:** F-Droid offers the update as usual, or install the APK below.
- **Desktop and server:** no changes since 1.6.0 beyond the version number. Desktop offers the update when it starts; the server's `Rekindle.Server --update` picks it up.

Every download except the Android APK has a `.sha256` next to it.
