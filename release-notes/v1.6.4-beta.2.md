**Pre-release.** The second beta of 1.6.4, bringing read aloud to Android. Includes everything from beta.1.

## New

- **Books read aloud (Android):** press the speaker button in a book's top bar and the book is read to you from the page on screen, using your phone's own text-to-speech voices. The sentence being read is highlighted, pages turn and scroll with it, and reading carries on into the next chapter. Pause, resume and stop from the top bar.
- **Keeps reading with the screen off (Android):** a notification and your lock screen give play, pause and stop controls. Reading pauses for calls and other audio, and when headphones are unplugged.
- **Read aloud from here (Android):** select some text and choose **Read aloud** to start there.
- **Voice and speed (Android):** pick a voice and a speed under **Read aloud** in Settings → Books or the reader's Reading options. **Phone's voice settings** opens the phone's text-to-speech settings, to add voices or another engine.
- Works in all four layouts, including continuous scroll.

## Notes for testers

This is the first Android build with read aloud, and it hasn't been tested widely on phones yet. Reports are welcome, especially about:

- pages not following the voice in the page and two-page layouts
- the continuous layout
- reading stopping with the screen off on phones with aggressive battery saving
- any chapter that now displays differently: chapters are now marked sentence by sentence for read aloud

Voices come from your phone's text-to-speech engine, so they vary from phone to phone. If you have no voices for offline use, add one in the phone's text-to-speech settings.

## Upgrading

- **Android:** install the APK below. On Android 13 and later, the first time you press play, Rekindle asks to show notifications, for the playback controls. Read aloud works either way.
- **Desktop:** no changes since beta.1 beyond the version number. Enable **Receive pre-releases**, then use Settings → Updates → **Check for updates**.
- **Server:** no changes since 1.6.3 beyond the version number.

Every download except the Android APK has a `.sha256` next to it.
