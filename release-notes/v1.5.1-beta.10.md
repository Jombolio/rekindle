**Pre-release.** The tenth beta of 1.5.1, including everything from earlier betas.

## New

- **Comic and manga pages turn like the book reader:** the hinged flip from beta.9 is now the book reader's paper curl. A fold sweeps from the page's free edge to its bound edge, the paper rolls over itself and shows its back, and the page beneath takes the roll's shadow. Right-to-left reading binds the sheet at the right edge and turns it the other way, and a swipe curls the page under your finger rather than snapping at the end. On both desktop and Android.
- **Page turn speed:** a new slider under Settings → Reader sets how long a page takes to turn, from 200 ms to 1400 ms. It covers comics and books on both clients, so the two readers stay in step. The default, 700 ms, is the book reader's existing turn.

## Changes

- **Comic page turns are slower by default:** a turn now takes 700 ms rather than 450 ms, matching the book reader. Use the new **Page turn speed** slider to set your own.
- **The curling page is the page, not the window:** the sheet is sized to the page as drawn, so the letterbox around it stays out of the turn.

## Upgrading

- **Desktop:** enable **Receive pre-releases**, then use Settings → Updates → **Check for updates**.
- **Android:** use Settings → Updates → **Check for updates** with pre-releases enabled, or install the APK.
- **Server:** no functional changes since beta.8. If upgrading from an earlier beta, update the server before the clients to use scheduled scans, read marks and tracking exclusions.

Every download except the Android APK has a `.sha256` next to it.
