Rekindle 1.6.0 is a big release for text books. They're rebuilt from the ground up: Kindle formats, streaming, page layouts, readability options, an offline dictionary, and on desktop, books read aloud in a natural voice. Comics and manga get paper-like page turns, and libraries get scheduled scans, read marks and type-to-search.

## Books

- **Text books open reliably and read properly** (desktop and Android). Images and the book's own styles show, links between chapters work, and paragraphs are indented as the book intends. Your place is kept within a chapter, including through font and theme changes, and a book only counts as finished near the end of its last chapter.
- **MOBI, AZW and AZW3 books can be read.** The server converts them to EPUB the first time they're opened, KF8 files included. DRM-protected books can't be opened, and the reader says why.
- **Books stream a chapter at a time**, like comic pages, so you can read without downloading first. Read-only accounts can now read text books; saving a whole book still needs the download permission.
- **Import Kindle books and EPUBs** (desktop, for accounts that can manage media). In the upload panel, pick a folder or a Kindle connected over USB to preview each book, upload the ready ones and see what was imported, skipped or failed. Rekindle doesn't remove DRM: protected and KFX books are reported and never uploaded.
- **Reading layouts.** Choose how a book is laid out:
  - **Scroll by chapter**, as before.
  - **Continuous scroll**, where the whole book is one scroll and each chapter follows the last.
  - **Single page**, a page at a time. On desktop, pages break between lines, never through one.
  - **Two pages**, a side-by-side spread that reads like a book: each chapter opens on a right-hand page, then pairs 2–3, 4–5 and so on.

  In the page layouts, turn pages by tapping or clicking either side of the page, with the arrow keys, Page Up/Down or Space, the mouse wheel, a swipe on Android, or the arrows in the bottom bar. Turning past a chapter's last page opens the next. Your place carries across layouts and devices.
- **Pages turn like paper** (desktop). A page curls from its edge with a curved fold, shading and a soft shadow; in a spread, the right-hand page folds over the spine onto the left. Turn off **Page-turn animation** for pages that change at once.
- **Readability fonts.** OpenDyslexic, Atkinson Hyperlegible and Lexend come with the apps, under the SIL Open Font License. Choose the default font and text size in Settings or from the reader.
- **Readability and accessibility options:** line, letter, word and paragraph spacing; line width; left or justified alignment; two high-contrast themes; bold text; and a reading ruler that keeps your place on the line you're reading. They're all in the reader's **Aa** panel and under Settings → Books, and changes apply straight away.
- **A default reading theme** (desktop and Android): Match app, Light, Dark or Sepia. The palette button in a book still changes it for that visit.
- **A built-in English dictionary** that works offline. On desktop, select a word; on Android, long-press a word and tap **Define**. The meaning floats over the page, so your place doesn't move.
- **Books read aloud** (desktop). Text books can be read to you in a natural voice, on your own computer. Download the voice once from Settings → Books (about 333 MB), then press the speech button in a book's top bar. The sentence being read is highlighted, pages turn with it, and reading carries on into the next chapter. Choose between three voices (Heart, Emma and George) in Settings or in the reader's Reading options, even mid-sentence. Works offline once downloaded. Not yet available in the continuous layout.

## Comics and manga

- **Pages turn like the book reader** (desktop and Android). A fold sweeps from the page's free edge to its bound edge, the paper rolls over and shows its back, and the page beneath takes its shadow. A swipe curls the page under your finger. Right-to-left reading binds the page at the right edge and turns it the other way. Turn off **Comic and manga page turns** under Settings → Reader to use sliding pages.
- **Double pages turn about the spine.** The half on the side you're turning from curls over the middle, carrying the next facing page on its back, while the other half stays down. Landscape pages and lone pages turn the same way.
- **Page turn speed:** a slider under Settings → Reader sets how long a turn takes, from 200 ms to 1400 ms, for comics and books on both clients. The default is 700 ms.

## Libraries

- **Scheduled library scans:** admins can set a scan interval for each library in Admin Panel → Scans, on desktop and Android.
- **Mark as read:** mark archives or whole folders as read without changing their saved reading position, and remove the mark whenever you want.
- **Ignore archive tracking:** exclude archives or folders from series progress, completion and new counts while keeping their own reading progress. Use the desktop right-click or Android long-press menu to track them again.
- **Type to search** (desktop): start typing while browsing a library or a series and the listing filters to match. Escape clears it. On by default; turn it off in Settings → Appearance.
- **Show file extensions:** an optional setting under Appearance on desktop and Android, off by default.

## Apps

- **Settings has categories** on desktop and Android: a landing page with separate pages for Appearance, Reader, Books, Downloads, Updates and more.
- **Android can check for updates.** Settings → Updates has **Check for updates**. Copies from F-Droid are still updated by F-Droid. Pre-releases aren't recommended on Android, since going back to a stable release means uninstalling first, so **Include pre-releases** takes three quick taps to turn on.
- **Android shows its version** beside "Rekindle" on the main screen.
- **Desktop: Escape goes back** from any screen, all the way to your libraries. Dialogs, menus and pop-ups close first.

## Fixes

- **Your reading progress sticks** (desktop). Closing the reader could save page 0 over your real progress, and closing a book and opening it again soon after could put you back where that visit started. Progress, comics included, is now saved as the reader closes.
- **In-app updates work for Windows installer installs.** Copies in Program Files said "This is a development build" unless run as administrator. They now offer **Update now**, which runs the installer and asks for admin rights as usual. A portable copy in a folder you can't write to says that's why it can't update itself.
- The update zip no longer stays locked on Windows after it's extracted.
- Maximising or restoring the window no longer leaves the comic reader black until the page is turned.

## Upgrading from 1.5.0

Update the server first: older servers can't stream books to these clients.

- **Server:** stop it, run `Rekindle.Server --update`, start it. Your `appsettings.json`, `data/` folder and other files are left untouched. The database gains new columns and tables on startup, for your place within a chapter, scan schedules, read marks and tracking exclusions, so back up `data/` if you might go back to 1.5.0.
- **Desktop:** Rekindle offers the update when it starts, or use Settings → Updates → **Check for updates**. On Linux, the AUR package and Flatpak now need sound (ALSA), for reading aloud.
- **Android:** F-Droid offers the update as usual, or install the APK below. F-Droid builds are signed differently from the APK, so switching between them means uninstalling first.
- **Coming from a 1.5.1 beta:** the server's `--update` and the desktop's automatic check both pick this release up.

Every download except the Android APK has a `.sha256` next to it.
